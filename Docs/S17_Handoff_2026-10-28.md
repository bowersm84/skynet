- **Plumbing first, reports second.** One company-wide **QuickBooks mirror** (customers, invoices, payments, credit memos, A/R and A/P aging, P&L, balance sheet) the way the Fishbowl bridge mirrors orders. Move April's and Sawyer's Friday exports onto the bridge where it already syncs that data. Reports then become queries plus a narrative.
- **Name the system of record:** Fishbowl = orders, shipments, product revenue (QuickBooks invoices are one lump-sum "Sales" line, numbered S+SO with the PO in a custom field); QuickBooks = invoices, payments, cash, P&L; SkyNet = production.
- **Parallel run** the weekly scorecard in SkyNet beside Cowork for 2–3 weeks; cut over when the numbers tie.
- **Saturday auto-send to leadership conflicts with the org rule** "work product is a draft until a person approves it" and with today's rule (review copy to Matt only). Proposed: SkyNet builds Saturday early, sends Matt the review copy, one click releases to leadership. Fully unattended release needs Matt to change the org rule deliberately.
- **Some inputs stay manual:** Truist Friday-close balances (unless the QuickBooks bank feed is reconciled), sales targets, the Giza batch schedule. Give them an upload slot and keep the "not provided — owner" behavior.
- **Month-end:** a SkyNet page with PDF export first; generate .pptx only if leadership needs to edit it.
- **Customer dashboards stay internal** (staff view of a customer's account). A customer-facing portal is a different security model; not now. Programs should be set up from a customer mapping, not hand-written seeds; email trail and Uncle Bob on per customer (Gmail reads and tokens per question).
- **Giza** keeps its product tracker; show it as a panel inside a Giza customer program.
